# Video Ideas: Innuendo Hooks for Teens

The formula: **a hook that sounds sus → the camera reveals something totally innocent → the viewer is the weird one.**
Teens get the joke, and the video stays clean, so it isn't flagged or age-restricted.

Each idea has a **Hook** (first 1–2 seconds, on-screen text and voiceover), the **Bit** (what happens), and the **Twist** (the innocent payoff).

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Parents Series

1. **"Ever wondered why your parents play patty cake EVERY NIGHT?!?!"**
   - Bit: Dramatic investigation music, you creep down the hallway at 11pm, ear on the door, hear clapping and heavy breathing.
   - Twist: You burst in. They're actually playing patty cake, and they're *competitive* about it. Dad: "BEST OF FIVE, LINDA."

2. **"Why does my dad say he's 'going to work on mom's back door' every Saturday?"**
   - Twist: Cut to Dad replacing the squeaky hinge on the actual back door. Three Saturdays in a row. It's still squeaking.

3. **"My parents told me to stay out of the house for 2 hours... so I hid in the closet 💀"**
   - Twist: They're doing taxes. Mom is crying. Dad is yelling at a calculator. You leave the closet traumatized for different reasons.

4. **"POV: You hear your mom yell 'IT'S TOO BIG, IT WON'T FIT' from the garage"**
   - Twist: Dad bought a Costco-sized pack of toilet paper and it won't fit in the cabinet.

5. **"Why do my parents always lock the door and say they're 'wrestling'?"**
   - Twist: WWE on TV, both in costume, Dad doing a full commentator voice. Mom hits him with a pillow from the top rope (the couch).

6. **"My mom asked my dad if he 'wants to get freaky tonight' 😳"**
   - Twist: She means trying the new spicy flavor of instant ramen. Dad is scared. Dad cries. Dad finishes the bowl.

## 🍆 Food Series (it's always food)

7. **"Rating every type of meat I've ever put in my mouth"**
   - Twist: Literal tier list of deli meats, but taken WAY too seriously. Bologna in F-tier with a 90-second rant.

8. **"How long can I keep it hard? (Day 47)"**
   - Twist: A loaf of bread you refuse to throw away. It's a weapon now. You knock on a wall with it.

9. **"Me trying to get it in on the first try"**
   - Twist: Threading a needle / throwing a bottle cap into a cup / shooting a paper ball into the trash. Fail compilation.

10. **"Grandma asked me to help her 'stuff the bird' and I wasn't ready"**
    - Twist: Thanksgiving turkey (very on-season). Grandma goes in elbow-deep with zero hesitation. You're the one who looks traumatized.

11. **"Things that are better when they're wet"**
    - Twist: Fast montage: water slides, wet wipes, cake (tres leches), a dog after a bath (no, it's not), sidewalk chalk art.

## 🏫 School Series

12. **"My teacher said 'pull it out' and the whole class went silent 💀"**
    - Twist: Reenactment: teacher meant "pull out a piece of paper." One kid in the back is just dying silently.

13. **"Why does the gym teacher always say 'I want it longer and harder'?"**
    - Twist: He's talking about the mile run. Kid is on the floor gasping.

14. **"The science teacher said we're going to study BALLS today"**
    - Twist: Physics lesson about gravity, dropping a bowling ball and a tennis ball. Teacher keeps saying "the balls hit the ground at the same time" while the class collapses.

15. **"When the substitute says 'I'll be coming every day this week'"**
    - Twist: Classroom freezes. One kid slowly turns to the camera. Cut to black. (Pure reaction bit.)

## 🧍 Everyday Life Series

16. **"I got caught doing it in the bathroom by my mom"**
    - Twist: Practicing a speech to the mirror / TikTok dance / pretending to be in a music video with a shampoo bottle mic.

17. **"How I get my girlfriend/boyfriend wet every time"**
    - Twist: Water gun ambush. Every time. Different locations. They're furious.

18. **"I spent 3 hours in my room touching myself... to see if I had a fever"**
    - Twist: Hypochondriac skit: thermometer, WebMD, writing a will. It's allergies.

19. **"The plumber said he's going to lay some pipe 🤨"**
    - Twist: He literally lays pipe. Long. Slow. Confident. Dramatic slow-mo. Mom watching from the kitchen nodding with respect.

20. **"My brother keeps saying he's 'beating it' in his room"**
    - Twist: The final boss of a video game. He's been beating it for 9 hours. He's been beating it since last week. He has NOT beaten it.

21. **"Just found out what my sister does with her 'friend' when she says 'Netflix and chill'"**
    - Twist: Actually watching Netflix. Both asleep 12 minutes in. Popcorn untouched.

22. **"I tried to blow it but it was too big"**
    - Twist: A giant beach ball / pool float / birthday balloon arch. Face turns purple.

## 🔁 Series Formats (easy to repeat)

23. **"Sus Sentences Out of Context"**: Record family members saying innocent things, cut so it sounds wrong, then show the full clip. ("Put it in slow, it's hot" → it was a lasagna.)

24. **"Explaining Innuendos to My Grandma"**: You say a phrase, Grandma takes it 100% literally and is very confused. *Bonus twist:* Grandma suddenly gets one and drops something way worse.

25. **"Only Teens Will Understand This Episode"**: Fake old kids' show clips (puppet, cartoon voice) with lines that are 100% innocent and 100% sus. "Today, Timmy learns how to stroke… a cat! 🐱"

---

## Hook Tips

- **First 1 second wins.** Put the sus line as big on-screen text right away. Don't build up to it.
- **Face reaction > words.** A slow turn to camera or a deadpan stare does half the comedy.
- **The twist should be *aggressively* innocent.** The more wholesome the reveal, the funnier it is.
- **End with the viewer being called out.** E.g. text at the end: *"what did YOU think it was? 🤨"*. That drives comments.
- **Caption bait:** "tag the friend whose mind went there first 💀"
- **Keep it implied, never explicit.** That's what keeps it funny, keeps it watchable for teens, and keeps the video from being age-restricted or taken down.
